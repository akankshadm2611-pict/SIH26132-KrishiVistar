# KrishiVistar

## Project Overview

* KrishiVistar is a digital platform designed to support farmers by bringing important agricultural information and services onto a single platform.
* It aims to improve farmers' access to market information, buyers, government schemes, agricultural resources, transportation, storage, and livestock-related services.
* The platform focuses on reducing information gaps and helping farmers make better decisions regarding their agricultural activities and produce.


## Proposed Solution

* KrishiVistar provides a centralized platform for different agricultural requirements.
* Farmers can access mandi rates and market information.
* Farmers can explore potential buyers and improve their market connectivity.
* The platform provides information about government schemes and agricultural assistance.
* It provides guidance related to crop damage and relief procedures.
* Farmers can find information related to livestock, transportation, and cold storage.
* The platform is designed to make agricultural information easier to discover and use.

##  Main Features

### Live Mandi Rates

* Provides information about agricultural commodity prices.
* Helps farmers compare market prices before selling their produce.
* Supports better price discovery and informed selling decisions.

### Farmer-Buyer Connection

* Provides a platform for farmers and buyers to connect.
* Helps farmers find potential buyers for their agricultural produce.
* Can improve direct market access and reduce dependency on intermediaries.

### Government Schemes

* Provides information about government schemes relevant to farmers.
* Includes information about agricultural subsidies, financial assistance, and support programs.
* Helps farmers identify schemes based on their requirements.

### Crop Damage Assistance

* Provides information about procedures related to crop damage.
* Helps farmers understand the process of reporting crop damage.
* Provides information about compensation and relief-related procedures.

### Livestock Support

* Provides resources related to cattle and livestock.
* Can include information about livestock schemes, management, and available support services.
* Helps farmers access livestock-related information from one platform.

### Transportation

* Provides information related to transportation of agricultural produce.
* Helps farmers identify transportation options for moving produce from farms to markets or buyers.
* Supports better movement of agricultural products.

### Cold Storage

* Provides information about cold-storage facilities.
* Helps farmers identify suitable storage options for perishable agricultural products.
* Can help reduce post-harvest losses.

### Agricultural Resources

* Provides access to different farming-related resources.
* Can include crop information, agricultural services, market information, and support resources.
* Brings multiple farmer requirements together on one platform.

##  Objectives

##  Working of the Platform

1. User opens the KrishiVistar platform.
2. User registers or logs in.
3. Farmer accesses the farmer dashboard.
4. Farmer selects the required service.
5. The platform displays the relevant information or available services.
6. Farmer can compare available information and opportunities.
7. Farmer can use the information to make more informed agricultural and marketing decisions.
